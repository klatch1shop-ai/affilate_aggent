 How to sell on ROZETKA
 Останнє оновлення: 12.06.2026

 Register as new seller

 To register as a new seller on ROZETKA Marketplace, please, visit our landing page and complete the registration form. We'll reach out to you. To complete the registration, you'll need to:

 Create a seller name.

 Sign a contract.

 After configuring your Seller account, you can use it to list and price products, manage your inventory, and fulfill customer orders. Do it on a web version, mobile app for Android or iOS. Also, you can configure data exchange with your system using API tools ↓ .

 Listing items in ROZETKA store

 Prepare information about your product, maintain an up-to-date assortment and competitive prices.

 Fulfill orders to the customers.

 You must fulfill orders yourself directly to customers in Ukraine and be able to receive payments. Payments are transferred directly to the seller using the method you have specified.

 Handle orders, contact customers, and track order statuses yourself. Do it quickly and efficiently to get positive feedback and build trust to your store.

 Pay referral fee

 ROZETKA offers a single seller plan: €8.33 per month plus a per-item referral fee that varies by category. Per-item referral fee charged for successfully completed orders only.

 Other costs may apply also if you decide to use optional tools and programs on ROZETKA Marketplace.
